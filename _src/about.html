Our story

Built around one detail most people overlook

Furniture is judged at its edges. A cabinet can use the best board on the market and still look cheap if the edge band is the wrong shade, peels or chips.

4Edge exists to get that detail right. We focus on quality, durability and attractive finishes, and offer wooden textures, matt finishes, fabric designs and high-gloss options that complement modern furniture and interiors.

Our goal is simple: reliable products, a wide range of shades and the same quality in every order, for furniture manufacturers, dealers and distributors across India.

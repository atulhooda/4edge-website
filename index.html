About 4Edge

The finishing line on every piece of furniture.

Prime Edge Polycraft LLP makes premium PVC edge banding under the 4Edge brand. We focus on quality, durability and good-looking finishes for the furniture and interior industry.

Edge banding is the first thing a hand touches and the first thing an eye notices. A shade that's slightly off or an edge that lifts can spoil a finished cabinet. We make sure 4Edge banding matches the panel, bonds cleanly and holds up for years.

You get reliable products, a wide range of shades and the same quality on every roll, whether you run a production line, stock a dealer counter or supply a region.
